## 📖 Project Overview

This end-to-end **Data Analytics** project explores smartphone addiction using **Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau**.

The project analyzes smartphone usage behavior, identifies the key factors contributing to smartphone addiction, and presents actionable insights through interactive dashboards and data storytelling.

# 📈 Key Insights

### 📌 Smartphone addiction is highly prevalent.

- **70.78%** of users were classified as addicted.

---

### 📌 Screen time is the strongest predictor.

Daily screen time showed the strongest relationship with smartphone addiction compared to all other variables.

---

### 📌 Weekend usage increases significantly.

| Metric | Average |
|---------|---------|
| Weekday Screen Time | **7.51 Hours** |
| Weekend Screen Time | **9.20 Hours** |

Users spend considerably more time on their phones during weekends.

---

### 📌 Higher addiction is associated with higher stress.

Users with severe smartphone addiction generally experience higher stress levels.

---

### 📌 Custom Risk Score (0–100)

A weighted Risk Score was developed using:

| Factor | Weight |
|---------|-------:|
| Screen Time | 30% |
| Sleep Deficit | 25% |
| Notifications | 20% |
| Stress Level | 15% |
| App Opens | 10% |

The score effectively identifies users at higher risk of smartphone addiction.

# ✅ Final Conclusion

Smartphone addiction is influenced by multiple behavioral factors rather than a single metric. While **screen time** is the strongest predictor, combining it with **stress levels, sleep quality, notifications, and app usage** provides a more comprehensive understanding of digital behavior.

This project demonstrates how data analytics, SQL, and business intelligence tools can transform raw behavioral data into actionable insights that support healthier digital habits.
